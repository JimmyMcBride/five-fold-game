# Fivefold Dungeon Authoring

Use for playable dungeon content.

## Principles

- Establish room purpose, sensory identity, meaningful exits, encounter pressure, and reward.
- Keep narration concise enough for repeated browser play.
- Write actionable environmental details, not lore walls.
- Cite canonical setting, bestiary, gear, or dungeon sources when reused.
- Label new balance values and authored content as adaptations until approved.
- Preserve player choice; do not hide mandatory outcomes behind decorative prose.
- Keep secrets in server-owned content projections when future content requires discovery.

St. Bozma’s Tomb is canonical example material, not automatically the first production dungeon. Plan must resolve that product choice.

# THE WORLD

`Fivefold’s world is the result of an apocalypse. Three hundred years ago, a mortal named Jirah rose to fame. He claimed to be a child of GRDN, the one and only god worshiped by the Moon Elves. His teachings and mission were controversial, as he came to reveal demons and defeat them for good - something that no one had ever been able to do before.` 

`Before Jirah, people were mostly powerless against the demons. People could fend them off using elaborate rituals and strike deals with them to placate their thirst for souls, but demons were rarely destroyed or overthrown. They acted from positions of power, and used their mortal puppets to rule from the shadows. When Jirah came, he began to undo the works of the Demonic horde, and freed those that suffered from their rule. He carried a blade that he never once unsheathed, and instead used his voice to root out evil and corruption. His mission amassed a very large following. Jirah’s crusade was based in The Holy City, ruled at the time by an oppressive, occupying empire of Sinistre Elves.`

`Both the Moon Elves in power over that region and their Sinistre overlords plotted to kill Jirah, as they were`   
`both in league with demons. They put him on trial for leading a political insurrection, and executed him on the Wheel - a terribly slow and painful death. Before his death, Jirah told his followers of a coming age of darkness that his people would have to fight back against. He warned, though few understood. Shortly after his death, this prophecy came to pass, and the sun set for the last time. The world went dark and was plunged into an eternal night, and the once subtle demons, no longer limited by the scorching sunlight, manifested in the suffocating darkness as a true horde. The nations of mortals were quick to divide and separate in the onset of panic. The world chilled into a deep freeze as the warmth was bled away in the sun’s absence, over half of the world died in the coming winter and famine.` 

`Now, some 300 years later, the world is covered in a permanent frost. People hide away from the demonic horde that wanders the wilderness, and the only bastions of civilization that are left are gathered around pockets of warmth: Coal mines, volcanos, hot springs, and the wandering flameclouds that are followed by nomadic tribes of Caprica. Travel between cities is dangerous, and so only the bravest and most prepared dare to venture forth.` 

# THE LEFT HANDED EMPIRE

`The empire of the Sinistre Elves once stretched from one end of the world to the other, and was by far the most powerful nation that the world had ever seen. It started in the hill countries of the south and grew into a massive state through fast-fought wars and conquering. The world saw a golden age of technology and culture thanks to the success of the empire, as more wealth flowed through the hands of its citizens than anyone thought possible.` 

- `Once ruled by a senate.`  
- `The senate was overthrown and an Emperor was crowned after a bloody civil war.`  
- `It ruled over almost the entire known world at its height, but never quite reached the lands that were furthest north, and it could not conquer the Hares that were east of the Holy City in their volcanic home.`  
- `After the Nightfall, the empire suffered another civil war that split it into an eastern and western half. The eastern half still exists, and is ruled by a queen. The western half fell apart, and the only thing that remains of it are ruins and its capital city, Sinistra.`   
- `Sinistra is still one of the most populated cities in the world thanks to its walls and ability to pull in resources from the surrounding lands. It has a strong standing army that protects it from invasions, and is the seat of a church that boasts considerable authority: Saint Lapishal’s Cathedral. Lapishal was one of Jirah’s followers - a moon elf man whose mission was to lead the newly blossoming order in its goals to provide light to a dark world.`  
- `The Sinistra Cisterns are a complex sewer system that runs deep beneath the city. It was once flowing with ever-renewing water that kept filth out of the city, but now is filled with vermin, monsters, and a coven of Occultists that do the bidding of the demon lord Ashtaroth.`

# THE SCORCHED

`Wandering tribes of nomads that follow the flameclouds. Mostly, they are made up of Caprica, but the tribes are fairly open to all species. Many tribes believe that the clouds are given to them by GRDN as an encouragement to live a more holy life in reliance on his words. Other tribes worship the clouds. Many believe the clouds contain angels, and some orders of paladins believe they contain demons. There are hundreds of different Scorched tribes, all with different beliefs and cultures.`

# RADIANT BLADES

`Spread throughout the world are blades forged of radiant metal. This metal is believed to be from the stars, cast to the earth during a war in the skies between GRDN’s angels and the first demons. Blades made from this sacred ore glow faintly, and are said to grant the wielder divine power at the cost of a shorter life. A candle that burns twice as bright burns twice as fast, after all. These blades have been forged and passed down for generations, and entire orders of knights have sprung up around the dogma of what it takes to be worthy of a radiant blade. The swords all have names, and they are only wielded by the most persistent of knightly souls.` 

`Example Radiant Blade: Excalibur`  
`Longsword. All weapon attacks with this longsword deal an additional d10 of damage, which cannot be resisted. When someone rests with this blade, they must pass a hard Heart roll or lose a recovery die.`

# THE SHIFTING RADIANCE

`In what was once the homeland of the Sphinxites, there is an ever shifting, glowing cloud that sickens whatever life dares to dwell there. This cloud rises from hundreds of glowing craters where orders of knights who carried Radiant Blades fought a civil war against one another after the fall of the Sinistre Empire. The glow warps and mutates life, but there is much of value still left in this exclusion zone for those brave enough to explore the wasteland in small doses. Monstrosities and undead wander the Radiance, and somewhere within the fog, an ancient king, older than time itself, watches from his fortress. He is only known as The Lich King. From his ancient mind came the dark arts of Vesselism: the practice of binding one’s soul to a new, inanimate body.`  
	`Vessels are most often powerful Magi, who have grown close to death due to age or sickness, and wish not to allow their mortal forms and souls to corrupt upon death into the demonic. The Lich King’s forbidden rites allow them to transfer their soul to something fitting - a suit of armor, a doll, a mannequin - by draining their blood through it in an excruciating ritual. The ritual does not always portend success, and failure is costly, as either the soul is split and left nearly mindless in the vessel and corrupted in the flesh, or they are left in severe pain that cannot be soothed, as if their final moments of agony are eternally perpetuated. Those that do manage to succeed are granted a much longer lifespan - potentially infinite.`

# ARGESH

`The volcanic homeland of the Hares. This place is still relatively warm, and will likely remain so for hundreds of thousands of years. The Hares protect it with a ferocity that kept the Sinistre Empire out centuries ago, and they don’t plan to stop now. Dinosaurs are commonplace in this region of the world, and some have even been domesticated by the Hares as steeds, beasts of burden or livestock.`

# ZARAGORVIN

`The Corvid homelands to the West. It was once a desert, and is almost entirely made of slot canyons and frozen dunes. The slot canyon’s unique shapes make it far easier to keep the snow out, and during blizzards, sheets of ice create a natural roof for shelter over the narrow ravines. The Corvids have discovered a way to make certain ice flammable using an art they call “Frostburning”. It is a lengthy process, and is only able to be properly done in any substantial quantity by the eldest masters of Frostburning that have spent their entire lives honing the craft. The flames of Frostburning emit no smoke, and burn in various shades of blue. It typically burns far colder than normal flame, but still hot enough to stave off the freeze and cook food over long periods. The greatest masters of the craft can overcome this limitation, however, and can produce blue flames that burn themselves out in superheated azure flashes.`

# LYSHEIM

`Lysheim is the homeland of the humans, far to the north across the ice sheets and naval graveyards. The humans here were once the most prolific naval power in the known world, but now, sailing has become quite hard. Oceans do still exist, on the furthest northern shores, but the seas that once separated these kingdoms from the mainland have frozen over into long ice bridges. No one knows why, but these former seas hold literal millions of ships, now permanently frozen in place. Many human communities have settled within more densely packed fleets and use these ships as their home. The citizens auger the ice here and fish the black seas below for food.` 

# THE PINES

`A collection of evergreen forests that still live, despite the cold and dark. These trees live due to being the seasonal homes and spawning grounds of the Skywhales, and are home to many clans of Sabertooth Elves that hunt the whales for their fat and meat. Some clans of elves ride large harpies through the sky on their hunts. Even before the nightfall, the Sabretooth Elves were known for their hunting prowess, and just after the sun went down, they made a deal with the humans from Lysheim to trade for large crossbows that fire harpoons. They’ve now perfected their craft, and can hunt skywhales better than any other group.`

# BEASTIARY

# **A few notes on enemy design:**

1. `Enemies all have a different Vice Type. This means they may deal more damage to characters with the matching Vice, or can inflict more Sin upon them with their abilities, or can cause specific effects on characters with a matching vice.`  
2. `Enemies grant the GM momentum just by being alive. At the end of round 1, the GM rolls 1d10 for each living enemy. On round 2, it goes up to 2d10/enemy, and on round 3, it goes up to 3d10/enemy.`  
3. `Enemies should have a ramp-up. This can be done by granting the GM more momentum with each passing round, that way they can access their more powerful maneuvers, or by simply giving maneuvers that can only happen on round 2, 3, 4, etc.`   
4. `Enemies will telegraph their worst attacks, that way the players have access to counter-play reliably. Each Enemy should be designed with a puzzle in mind. Weaknesses that reward strategic gameplay, and strengths that punish player characters that do not engage in the counter-play. As long as it is properly telegraphed, this should be fine.`  
5. `Enemies will scale into rather large numbers fairly quickly. Player power creep is currently quite strong thanks to scrolls and perks, so the toughest enemies should be able to handle that.`  
6. `By Default, enemies can only make a weapon attack or use an ability on their turn, unless their stat block indicates otherwise.`   
7. `When the GM reaches 30 momentum, they pick a maneuver from one of the enemies that is in the fight.`

**`NEW MOMENTUM RULES`**  
`At the start of each round, the GM rolls a number of d10s for each monster, which is listed in each Monster’s stat block next to their name. On any roll of a 10, the GM gets to use a monster’s maneuver on that monster’s turn. Additional 10s in the pool can either trigger other monster’s maneuvers or grant a monster an extra weapon attack on their turn, or sometimes empower a monster’s maneuver.`

# ANGELS

`Angels are the hosts of the heavenly court, and servants of GRDN’s will. They are, by and large, the most reliable source on the will of the sleeping god. They are beings crafted of pure light, and they hate two things: Demons, and sin. Angels, as capable as they are, are often focused only on a single task, and will almost never venture out from their oath’s purpose to do anything extra. They are robotic, and cold, and communicate efficiently, using as few words as possible (which is sometimes zero) to get their point across. Slaying angels is a sort of cosmic wrong, and when an angel is slain, all characters that damaged it during that combat encounter gain sin, as shown on each angel’s stat block.`

`Level 3 Angel (Wrath)`  
`Sin gained: 3`  
`Size: 4`  
`Health: 280`  
`Reflex: 74`

`Resistant to Holy and Dark damage; Sharp and Blunt from weapons that don’t have any scrolls.`  
`Immune to Poison Damage and effects caused by poison features.`

*`Thrones are the protectors of sacred places and saints. They are often oath-bound to show up when certain shrines are threatened, or when a holy place is being defiled. * **`Maneuver: Exile.`** `The throne attempts to Banish a target that is taunted by its Temple Guardian ability. The taunted target must make a Soul Roll or become banished until the end of their own next turn, upon which they take 1d10 flame damage. Additional 10s on the momentum die can be spent to add an additional d10 to the damage.`    
* **`Weapon Attack: Radiance.`** `2d10 + 7 Holy, Far.`  
* **`Ability: Temple Guardian.`** `The Throne forces itself in front of a creature. The target must roll Soul or become taunted until the end of the Throne’s next turn. Targets that are taunted by this ability are vulnerable to holy damage. Wrath viced characters have disadvantage on the roll to resist this ability.`

# **HELLHORNET (1d10)**

`Level 1 Beast (Wrath)`  
`Size: 1`  
`Health: 20`  
`Reflex: 7`

`Vulnerable to Flame Damage.`

*`Hellhornets are carnivorous, cobalt-blue winged insects. They can grow to be about the size of a large dog, and they hunt small animals by swarming them and stinging them until they can no longer move. Once their prey has been subdued, they take it back to their papery hive - which is often made in an old house, tower, or church building - and store their prey in a thick, acidic liquid. This liquid is called “hornet jelly”, and is harvested by brave apiarist that tend to domesticated hives. This jelly is sweet and savory, and can be made into mead, a spread for bread, or eaten raw.`*

`Hellhornets will attack when provoked, and always fight to the death. They usually attack in groups of 4 or more, and prefer the smallest targets. If they manage to paralyze a creature with their Sting ability, the hellhornets will attempt to take the creature to their hive with their Back to the Hive maneuver. If they manage to do this, the player characters might have to chase the hornets back to the hive for a rescue mission. A character loses 1 Recovery Die for each hour they are submerged in hornet jelly.`

* **`Maneuver: Back to the Hive`**`. The Hellhornets begin to take a target back to their hive to be turned into jelly. Hellhornets will only ever take creatures of size rank 2 or lower, and it takes 1 hornet for each size rank. If there is a target that has been paralyzed by their Sting ability, they use this maneuver and flee with it.`  
* **`Weapon Attack: Cutting Wings`**`. 1d10 + 4 Sharp, Far.`  
* **`Ability: Sting`**`. The Hellhornet makes a weapon attack that deals 4 poison damage on a hit. Each additional sting repeats the damage from all previous stings on the target (e.g. if 3 stings have already damaged the target, the next sting will deal 12 poison damage because it is the fourth sting). If a character is reduced to 5 health or lower by a sting, the target is Paralyzed by the venom for 1d10 hours. This can be nullified by anything that ends effects caused by poison.`

Some say they are primordial titans, others claim that they are each a lord of a layer of Hell. Others still claim them to be leftovers from a previous world, now driven mad by this new reality.`

# **RELGATH, THE IGNEOUS SCORPION (10d10)**

`Level 20 Beast (Gluttony)`  
`Size: 10`  
`Health: 2520`  
`Reflex: 2`

`- Immune to Flame damage (Magma Form and Armorless)`  
`- Resistant to Blunt damage, Immune to Sharp damage, Vulnerable to Flame Damage (Stone Form)`

*`A gargantuan scorpion, larger than most castles. Relgath wanders the volcanic ashlands of Argesh on a never-ending hunt. The beast feasts upon stilt crabs, triceratops, and has even been seen preying on the fearsome Tyrannosaurus Rex. Relgath’s favorite food, though, seems to be something found within the leylines of the earth, deep in the volcanic veins. The monster finds ancient temples and druidic circles and digs monumental burrows beneath them. Occultists flock to these burrows when Relgath rests there, to siphon away the arcane energy that flows from the earth in his unknowable feast. Relgath’s cavernous burrows become littered with veins of a valuable ore called erudite.`* 

`4 claw attacks and 1 Ability per turn. Relgath prefers to target the character with the highest maximum health, as they look like the best meal. His stinger targets the character that dealt the most damage to him on the previous round. If Relgath is reduced to 220 health, he will attempt to flee by using his burrow maneuver, in which he will flee to his lair. While in his lair, he regains 100 hit points per day. He will not flee from his lair, though, and will fight to the death there. GMs are encouraged to make this a fully fleshed out dungeon.` 

`Loot: ~10 Erudite Ore. Erudite ore is worth 350 gp each, and can be forged into Erudite weapons and armor.`

* **`Passive: Three Forms.`** `Relgath has three forms with drastically different abilities: Magma Form, Stone Form and armorless.`   
  **`Magma Form:`** `When Relgath burrows into the ground, magma sticks to his form and becomes flaming armor. Characters that end their turn in the near rank take 5 Flame damage.`   
  **`Stone Form.`** `Three Rounds after burrowing, the magma on Relgath’s form hardens into stone. While in this form, he loses two weapon attacks, but gains 100 temporary hit points. When these hitpoints are gone, he becomes Armorless. Every time he loses 25 of these temporary hit points, some of the stone falls off, and can be harvested as a single erudite ore.`  
  **`Armorless.`** `Relgath has no extra armor covering his chitin. While in this form, he takes 1d10 additional damage from all sources, but can use his stinger ability twice per turn instead of only once.`  
* **`Maneuver: Obliteration beam`**`. (Magma or Stone Form only) Relgath shoots a concentrated beam from beneath the cracks of his armor. All characters must make a Reflex or Heart Defensive roll, suffering the effects on a failure. While in Magma form, it deals 3d10+10 Flame damage. While in Stone form, it does 1d10 poison damage instantly, and 4d10 poison damage at the end of an affected character’s next turn. Additional 10s on the momentum die can be spent to increase the damage of these beams by an additional d10.`  
* **`Maneuver: Burrow`**`. (Armorless only) Relgath burrows beneath the ground to recoat himself in armor. He submerges himself, and becomes Banished until the end of his next turn. When he appears, he is in Magma form. If Relgath flees during this turn, the players can not roll to stop it.`  
* **`Weapon Attack: Claws`**`. 4d10 + 20 Sharp, Far.`  
* **`Ability: Stinger`**`. Relgath makes a weapon attack against a single character. If this attack hits, it deals 15 poison damage instantly, and the character must Hard succeed on a Heart Defensive Roll. On a failure, they are Dazed and will take 15 poison damage at the end of their next turn.`

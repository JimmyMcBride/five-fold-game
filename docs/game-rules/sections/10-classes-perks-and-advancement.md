# CLASSES, PERKS & ADVANCEMENT

`There are Five starting classes in Fivefold, with each being associated with one of the five stats: Warrior (Heart), Scout (Reflex), Priest (Soul), Magi (Mind), and Versant (Voice). When you create a character, pick one of these classes, and consult their entry in this section to see what you gain.`

# **ADVANCEMENT**

`Your GM will sometimes tell you to level up, typically after a climactic story beat. Whenever you do so, mark down your new level on your character sheet, choose a new perk that you meet the stat requirements for, and add 5 points to one attribute. Then, roll a d100 for every other stat. If this d100 roll is above the stat you rolled for, then it increases by 5. The maximum level for any character is 10.` 

`In addition, you will increase your maximum health by 1d10 + your Heart Modifier.` 

#### **`ALTERNATIVE RULE: EXPERIENCE-BASED ADVANCEMENT`**

`Rather than advancing your levels based on story beats, characters can level up by attaining victory in combat. With this method, when the players achieve victory in combat, add up the level of each enemy in a combat encounter, and divide it by the number of characters in the party. Each player character gains that much experience, rounded down. In order to advance to the next level, a character must gain 10 x their current level.` 

# **SPECIALIZATION**

`Whenever you level up and meet the requirements for picking a specialization, you can do so. Thematically, each of the specializations is a combination of your starting class and the class associated with the stat you are specializing into. Consult your classes entry in this section to see your options. When you specialize, you do not gain a perk from leveling up, as your specialization will give you an Origin perk.` 

# WARRIOR

`Warriors choose one Origin perk from Heart, and gain the following features:`

**`End Them Rightly.`** `(Maneuver) All enemies that are currently Taunted or Stunned by you take additional damage from all sources until the end of your next turn. The additional damage is equal to your level.`  
**`Hold Aggression.`** `(Action) Any enemy that is currently taunted by you remains taunted until the end of your next turn.`   
**`Threatening Strike.`** `(Action) Make a weapon attack against one enemy. If the attack hits, it only deals damage equal to half your Heart Modifier, and the enemy is taunted or stunned until the end of your next turn.`  
**`Eye for an Eye.`** `(Ability) You make a weapon attack. If the target damaged you on their last turn, or is stunned, the attack deals double damage.`  
**`Know thy Weapon.`** `(Passive) Choose one type of Heart Weapon (claymore, mace, kopesh, etc). When you deal damage with that weapon type, you gain 1 extra momentum.`  
**`Live for Battle.`** `(Passive) When you are targeted by a maneuver, ability or weapon attack, you gain 1 momentum.`

*`Flavor text. The charismatic, able to encourage their allies and discourage their enemies. They are merchants, bards, diplomats, and can call down fire from heaven, or stoke the fire within.`*

`Versants choose one Origin perk from Voice, and gain the following features:`

**`Encouragement`**`. (Maneuver) You always seem to know what to say, even under pressure. Until the end of your next turn, you can turn one roll that an ally failed into a success. If no ally failed a roll during that time, roll a d10, and immediately gain momentum equal to the number rolled.`  
**`Hushing Flame.`** `(Action) Roll Voice, and on a success, you deal Flame damage equal to your Voice modifier to one enemy. On a Hard or Critical success, they are also Silenced until the end of your next turn.`   
**`Curse`**`. (Action, Sin +1) You roll Voice, staunching an enemy’s ability to generate momentum. On a success, that enemy does not add momentum die to the GM’s pool.`  
**`Bless`**`. (Ability) You speak a blessing over an ally. Roll a d10, and one target of your choice lowers all of their rolls by the number you rolled until the end of your next turn.`  
**`Negotiator`**`. (Ritual) Once per day, you can push your luck with people. When you roll Voice in the context of a social situation (bribing, convincing, intimidating, lying, bartering, etc), you can turn a failed roll into a Normal Success, a Normal Success into a Hard Success, and a Hard Success into a Critical Success.`  
**`Tough Crowd`**`. (Passive) You gain 1 momentum for each enemy that is still in combat at the end of your turn, up to a maximum of your Voice Modifier.` 

## **SPECIALIZATIONS**

#### **`JESTER`**

*`Requirement: 80 Voice`* 

`When you become a Jester, you choose an Origin perk from Voice and gain the following features:`

**`Insult to Injury`**`. (Passive) When you succeed on a Defensive Roll against an enemy and the result on the die is an even number, you can mock the enemy for their failures. Depending on the number, you afflict the enemy in one of the following ways:`

* **`Result ends in 0`**`: The enemy is Silenced until the end of your next turn.`  
* **`Result ends in 2`**`: Your allies deal additional damage equal to half your Voice Modifier to the enemy until the end of your next turn.`  
* **`Result ends in 4`**`: The GM rolls less momentum die on the next round. The number the roll is reduced by is equal to your Voice Modifier.`   
* **`Result ends in 6`**`: You or one ally of your choice gains momentum equal to your Voice Modifier.`  
* **`Result ends in 8`**`: All attacks against the enemy until the end of your next turn are rolled twice, with the better result being taken.`

**`Mocking Taunt`**`. (Ability) You Taunt one enemy until the end of your next turn. When you make defensive rolls against this enemy, the roll is lowered by an amount equal to twice your Voice Modifier.`  
**`The Best Medicine`**`. (Ritual) Your entertainment and jesting raises the spirits of your company. Laughter is, of course, the best medicine for one’s heart. Once per day, when you take a rest, all who roll their Recovery Die recover additional hit points equal to twice your Voice Modifier.`

#### **`SWORD SINGER`**

*`Requirement: 80 Heart`*

`When you become a Sword Singer, you choose an Origin Perk from Heart, and gain the following features:`

**`Burning Heart.`** `(Maneuver) Your song empowers your allies and lifts their spirits. Until the end of your next turn, you and any allies on your rank deal additional flame damage equal to your Heart modifier with their weapon attacks.`  
**`Afterburn.`** `(Ability) You chant a verse that sets the wounds you inflict ablaze with molten fury. Any enemy that has taken Flame damage since the start of your last turn takes Flame damage equal to your Heart modifier.`  
**`Fan the Flames.`** `(Passive) When you use a maneuver, you regain 2 momentum.`

#### **`PSALMIST`**

*`Requirement: 80 Soul`*

`When you become a Psalmist, you choose an Origin perk from Soul and gain the following features:`

**`Psalm`**`. (Maneuver) You begin to play a song. You continue playing the song as long as you use your Action to continue concentrating on it, and your concentration is not broken, or until you choose to stop. You can change the song you are playing by using this maneuver again. Choose which song you play from the list below:`

* **`Song of Praise`**`. You and your allies heal for an additional amount equal to your Soul Modifier whenever you are healed.`   
* **`Song of Protection`**`. You and your allies take less damage from all sources equal to half your Soul Modifier.`   
* **`Song of Strength`**`. You and your allies deal additional damage with weapon attacks equal to half your Soul Modifier.` 

**`Selah`**`. (Maneuver) Roll Soul , and on a success, one enemy of your choice is both stunned and silenced until the end of your next turn.`  
**`Reprise`**`. (Ability) You make a Soul roll. On a success, you perform one of the following, depending on what song you are playing:`

* **`Praise`**`. You restore 1d10 health to a target of your choice.`   
* **`Protect`**`. One target of your choice has advantage on their next defensive roll.`  
* **`Strengthen`**`. An ally of your choice is able to make a single weapon attack.`

#### **`SERPENT`**

*`Requirement: 80 Reflex`*

`When you become a Serpent, you choose an Origin Perk from Reflex, and gain the following features:`

**`Fiery Constrictors`**`. (Maneuver) Your poisons burn and slow the flow of blood in your enemies. When you use this maneuver, all instances of poisons from Plaga Serpentis are removed, and any enemies that were affected by Plaga Serpentis are bound until the end of your next turn. Any weapon attack that damages an enemy bound in this way deals additional 2d10 flame damage.`  
**`Plaga Serpentis`**`. (Ability) Roll Reflex, and on a success, you poison an enemy. Any time a poisoned enemy is damaged by a weapon attack, they take 1d10 additional poison damage, which benefits from your Coagulating Torment passive, no matter who damages the enemy.`   
**`Coagulating Torment`**`. (Passive) When you roll poison damage, rolling an 8-10 on the die causes it to deal even more damage, allowing you to roll another d10, and add it to the damage. If the additional die shows an 8-10, you can roll yet another, and so on.`

#### **`DIPLOMAT`**

*`Requirement: 80 Mind`*

`When you become a Diplomat, you choose an Origin Perk from Mind, and gain the following features:`

**`Silver-Tongued`**`. (Maneuver) You convince all the enemies that are currently Pacified to side with you for a moment. One target takes damage from the pacified enemies' weapon attacks.`  
**`Pacify`**`. (Ability, 1 Momentum) You can attempt to pacify an enemy. Roll Mind, and if you roll a Hard Success, they will not target you with any hostile maneuver, ability, or action until the end of your next turn. If you speak their native language, the roll only needs to be a Normal Success. If you use an ability, action, or maneuver that deals damage to them, they are no longer Pacified. If you manage to keep them pacified for three rounds, they may become Enthralled, leaving the fight willingly or even join your side, according to the GM's decision.`  
**`Unhand me!`** `(Action, 1 Momentum) You sass, back-hand, or lightly slap the cheek of a foe. Roll Mind, and on a success, one pacified creature needs one less round to become Enthralled. If you target a creature that is not pacified, you have advantage on defensive rolls against that creature’s weapon attacks until the end of their next turn.`  
**`Linguist`**`. (Passive) You know two more languages.`

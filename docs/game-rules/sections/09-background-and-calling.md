# BACKGROUND & CALLING

# **BACKGROUND**

`When you create a character in Five Fold, consider who they were before the start of the campaign. Where they came from, what they did as a living, what their upbringing was like - all of that should play into who your character is now.` 

`Rather than picking from a list of backgrounds, you have three Background spots on your character sheet. These three can be anything suitable to a background, like "Hermit", "Blacksmith", "Pilgrim", and can even be locations your character grew up in, or hobbies they may have had. Whenever you roll something that has to do with one of your Backgrounds, you roll that check twice and take the better of the two results, as your character is more likely to succeed at tasks that they are familiar with.`

## **Crafting?**

`A player may choose to play a character with a crafting background, like a blacksmith, tailor, or an alchemist, and they may want to create items during their journey. Typically, crafting components for any item can be bought for half the price of the finished item (or less, if it seems appropriate to the GM), and a player can craft an item while resting, as long as they have the appropriate tools. Typically, the tools will be in a town or city, but an alchemist or tailor might be able to bring their profession’s tools with them on the road if they have the room in their inventory. The GM can have the character make a number of background rolls, and with an appropriate number of successes, the character crafts the item. The player and GM should collaborate on how long this process takes, how many successes they might need, etc, with respect to the difficulty of the craft and the tools available.`

# **CALLING**

`All characters in Fivefold have a Calling. This is their longterm goal that they are looking to one day achieve. The calling can be derived from the character's backstory and vice, and is how they overcome their vice. It should be something based on GM and player discussion, with themes and story beats included in the adventure of the character. When a character completes their calling, they can choose to discard their vice and replace it with any two perks of their choice. These perks can from any tree, any class or subclass, and a character does not need to meet the prerequisites for it.`

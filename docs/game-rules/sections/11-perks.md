# PERKS

`When you level up, you are able to pick a perk from one of the stats.`

# **HEART**

#### **`Origin Perks:`**

**`Knight`**`. (Passive) When you successfully roll Heart as a defensive roll, the damage you take is further reduced by 1 for each piece of Heavy armor equipped.`  
**`Bastion`**`. (Ability) When you use this ability, choose one of your allies. Until the end of your next turn, each time they take damage, Roll Heart. On a success, you shield them from some of the damage. The damage they take is halved, and you take the other half.`  
**`Iron Resolve`**`. (Passive) When you successfully roll Heart as a defensive roll, you gain 1 momentum.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 35`**

**`Nourished`**`. (Passive) While Well Fed, all of your rolls are decreased by 5.`  
**`Provoke`**`. (Ability) You Taunt one enemy until the end of your next turn, forcing them to target only you with Weapon Attacks.`  
**`Hold the Line`**`. (Ability) Until the end of your next turn, you take 1 less damage for each ally on the Near Rank.`  
**`Tempered as Gold`**`. (Passive) When you are suffering from a negative condition, all of your rolls are lowered by an amount equal to your Heart Modifier.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 45`**

**`Relentless Endurance`**`. (Passive) When you reach your Momentum Threshold, you gain Temporary health equal to your Heart Modifier.`  
**`Shield Wall`**`. (Ability) Until the end of your next turn, allies in the Far Rank lower their defensive rolls by 20.`  
**`Prayer of the Berserker`**`. (Ritual) You meditate on your own mortality, and use doom to empower your fury. If you have more than half of your total hit points, you drop to half and cannot be healed above half your total hit points until the start of the next day. While below half your total hit points, you deal extra damage with your weapon attacks equal to your Heart Modifier.`  
**`Shockwave`**`. (Maneuver) You stomp the ground and crack the cold earth beneath you. Roll Heart against each enemy in the Near Rank. Each target you succeed against is Stunned until the start of your next turn.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 55`**

**`Chain Pull`**`. (Ability). You use a chain, physical or spectral, to attempt to grab an enemy and pull them to you. Roll Heart, and on a success, the target is pulled from the Far Rank to the Near Rank, and is Bound for one turn.`  
**`Bloodied but Unbroken`**`. (Passive) While below half your total hit points, you lower your Heart Defensive Rolls by 10.`  
**`Brotherhood`**`. (Ritual) Choose one ally. You bond with them, and together, you both become more effective in combat. They deal 1 more damage to enemies that you have Taunted, and you gain 1 momentum each time an enemy damages them.`  
**`Strong Frame`**`. (Passive) You gain additional inventory slots equal to half your Heart Modifier.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 65`**

**`Shining Plate`**`. (Passive) All bonuses granted by Scrolls that are slotted into your equipped heavy armor are increased by 1.`  
**`Aegis Raised`**`. (Ability) You immediately gain temporary Hit points equal to half your Heart stat. These temporary hit points last until the end of your next turn. You can use this ability once per combat.`  
**`Seared Numb`**`. (Ability, Sin +2) Until the end of your next turn, all incoming damage is reduced by an amount equal to your Sin. Each successive turn that you use this ability in a row, the Sin gain increases by 2. You cannot use this ability while your Sin is above your total Recovery Dice.`   
**`Bleeding, but not dead.`** `(Passive) When you begin Dying, your weapon attacks have advantage, and they deal 1d10 additional damage. In addition, using an action, ability, or maneuver does not cause you to lose a Recovery Die.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 75`**

**`Standard Bearer`**`. (Ability) You raise the mark of your companions in triumph. You and all allies gain 2 Momentum.`  
**`Name thy Blade`**`. (Ritual) Choose one weapon to create a powerful bond with. You conduct a ritual and name your weapon, and while you wield it, all Scrolls slotted into the weapon are twice as powerful.`  
**`Sacrifice`**`. (Passive) When an ally is reduced to 0 hit points in combat, you can instead interpose yourself to take the blow instead. You take the damage they would have taken, and cannot roll defensively to reduce or avoid the damage.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 85`**

**`Avalanche Charge`**`. (Maneuver) The Blizzard quakes at your advance, and rises beneath you to level the battlefield. When you use this Maneuver, all enemies take Cold damage equal to your Heart and are forced onto the Near Rank.`  
**`Last Stand`**`. (Passive) The first time you would drop to 0 hit points in combat, you instead drop to 1 hitpoint and gain temporary hit points equal to your Heart stat.`  
**`The Largest Threat`**`. (Ability) You Taunt every enemy on the Near Rank until the end of your next turn.`

# **SOUL**

#### **`Origin Perks:`**

**`Fasting and Prayer`**`. (Passive) When you are Hungry, Soul rolls are decreased by 10.`  
**`No Weapon Formed Against Me`**`. (Passive) You can use your Soul stat instead of Reflex for defensive rolls.`  
**`Sanctified`**`. (Passive) While you have no Sin, all of your modifiers increase by 1.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 35`**

**`Summon Seraphim.`** `(Ability, Summon) You summon an angelic being. When it appears, it removes one condition and 1 Sin from you or an ally by touching holy embers to the target's lips. On your turn, it can use the Divine Flames ability, which deals flame damage equal to your Soul Modifier to one enemy. It can only remove Sin from the same target once per combat.`   
**`Prayer of Healing.`** `(Ability) You pray over yourself or an ally to heal wounds. Roll Soul, and if you are successful, you heal for 1d10 + your Soul Modifier. If you fail this roll, you heal for your Soul Modifier, but cannot use this ability again until the next day. You must be on the same rank as your target when you use this ability.`  
**`Truth of Mercy.`** `(Passive) At the start of the day, your Sin is removed.`  
**`Oath against Bloodshed.`** `(Passive) Your attack rolls with weapons that deal blunt damage are lowered by an amount equal to your Soul Modifier.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 45`**

**`Lay on Hands.`** `(Passive) When you heal yourself or an ally, one other character on the same rank is healed for half the amount.`  
**`Radiant Call.`** `(Ability) You call forth and manifest celestial judgement with a bolt of light. Roll Soul against a target, and on a success, they take Holy damage equal to your Soul Modifier, and attack rolls against that target are lowered by the same amount until the end of your next turn.`  
**`Quickened by Spirit.`** `(Passive) When you heal an ally that is below half their total health, you gain 2 momentum.`  
**`Guardian Angel.`** `(Passive) When there is an allied Angel in combat, one creature of your choice is granted Temporary health equal to your Soul Modifier at the start of each of your turns.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 55`**

**`Manna`**`. (Ritual) Once per day, you can pray for GRDN to provide a meal. Roll Soul, and on a success, food appears at your feet. This produces enough food to feed a number of characters equal to your Soul Modifier.`  
**`Sacred Covenant`**`. (Passive) When you heal an ally, they take less damage from all sources equal to your Soul Modifier until the end of your next turn.`  
**`Summon Ophanim.`** `(Ability, Summon) You summon an Angel. When it appears, it makes a number of creatures equal to your Soul Modifier bound until the end of your next turn. On your turn, it can use the Bound On Earth ability, which silences an enemy until the end of your next turn.` 

#### **`Prerequisite: 65`**

**`Prayer of Forgiveness`**`. (Ability or Ritual) Once per day, you can pray to GRDN for forgiveness for either you or an ally. The character removes all Sin.`  
**`Stigmata`**`. (Ability) You take on the wounds of Jirah and bleed his cleansing blood. When you use this Ability, you can take damage up to your Soul modifier, and either remove sin from yourself equal the amount, or deal Holy damage to one target equal to double the amount.`  
**`Sanctuary`**`. (Maneuver) You sanctify an ally and protect them. The first time they take damage after you use this Maneuver, the damage is halved.`  
**`Sin Eater`**`. (Ritual) Once per day, per character, You can take on half the Sin of a willing creature. Their Sin is halved, and you gain the amount that they lost.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 75`**

**`Remission.`** `(Passive) When sin is removed from you in combat, you gain 3 momentum.`  
**`Seraphic Choir.`** `(Passive) While you have an angel summoned, All allies in your rank have their critical chance increased by 10, and are immune to Fear.`  
**`Consuming Fire`**`. (Ability) All enemies on one rank take Flame damage equal to 2d10 + your Soul Modifier. This ability deals double damage to Demons.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 85`**

**`Summon The Angel of GRDN.`** `(Ability, Summon) You summon an angel of Salvation. When it appears, it removes all debuffs from your allies and ends all effects applied by Sin, assuming your allies are willing. On your turn, it can use the Day of the Lord ability, dealing Holy damage equal to half of your Soul Stat to all enemies. Once it has used the Day of the Lord ability three times, it disappears. You can summon this Angel once per day.`  
**`Raise the Dead.`** `(Ritual) Once per day, you can resurrect a character that has been dead for no longer than a week.`  
   
**`Celestial Mandate.`** `(Ability) You briefly tap into God's divine creation and create a bridge between the mortal realm and Elysium. For the duration of combat, the Momentum threshold for yourself and allies is reduced by 5, and you and your allies take less damage from all sources equal to your Soul Modifier. This ability can be used once per day.`

# **REFLEX**

#### **`Origin Perks`**

**`Skirmisher`**`. (Passive) When you make an attack against an enemy that has been attacked by one of your allies since the end of your last turn, you deal additional damage equal to your Reflex modifier.`  
**`Steady Aim`**`. (Passive) Your weapon attacks have their critical range increased by your Reflex modifier x 2.`  
**`Tactical Reposition`**`. (Action, 3 momentum) You switch ranks and gain 3 momentum.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 35`**

**`Retreat`**`. (Passive) When you switch from the Front Rank to the Back Rank, you gain 1 momentum.`  
**`Sharpshooter`**`. (Ability) Until the end of your next turn, your ranged weapon attacks have advantage.`  
**`Lucky Shot`**`. (Passive) When you succeed on an attack roll with a reflex weapon and the result is an even number, you deal 1 additional damage.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 45`**

**`Deadly Critical`**`. (Passive) Your critical hits deal an additional damage equal to your Reflex modifier.`  
**`Ricochet`**`. (Maneuver) If wielding a ranged weapon, you can make an attack that may bounce between your enemies. Make an attack roll, and on a hit, you can choose another enemy to make an attack roll against. On a hit, they take damage equal to your Reflex modifier, and you can pick yet another enemy. The projectile continues to bounce until you fail an attack roll, but an enemy cannot be targeted more than once.`  
**`Poison`**`. (Ability) Your next attack that hits deals Poison damage equal to your Reflex modifier.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 55`**

**`Extra Attack`**`. (Passive) When you use your action to attack with a Reflex weapon, you can make two weapon attacks instead of one. You do not gain more momentum when you make this attack.`  
**`Hide`**`. (Maneuver) You switch ranks and disappear into the chaos of battle. You are hidden until you use an action, ability, or maneuver that harms an enemy.`  
**`Precision`**`. (Ability) Once per day, you make the critical chance for your Reflex weapons increase by 20. This lasts until the end of the current combat encounter.`  
**`Poison Dart`**`. (Action) You carry poison-tipped darts on you. These count as Reflex weapons that deal 1d10 Poison damage, with the following properties: One Handed, Far, 1 Momentum.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 65`**

**`Thieve`**`. (Action) Roll Reflex. On a success, you steal the highest rolled Momentum die from your enemies when the GM next rolls their momentum pool. You gain the number shown on that die.`  
**`Set Trap`**`. (Ability) You set a trap amongst the ranks of your enemies. The first enemy to switch ranks, willing or not, is caught by the trap and takes 2d10 + your Reflex Modifier damage. When you set the trap, you decide if the trap does sharp or poison damage.`  
**`Lingering Venom`**`. (Ability) You immediately cause any enemy that has taken poison damage from you since the start of your last turn to take Poison damage equal to your Reflex Modifier.`  
**`Pack Light`**`. (Passive) If you have half or less of your inventory slots filled, your Reflex Rolls are reduced by 5.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 75`**

**`Marked for Death`**`. (Maneuver) When you use this maneuver, choose an enemy. The next time you hit them with a weapon attack, it is a Critical hit.`  
**`Parry`**`. (Passive) While duel wielding, your defensive rolls against weapon attacks have advantage.`  
**`Serpent's Aura`**`. (Action) Your poisons can spread like a cloud of toxic gas. Any enemy that has taken poison damage from you since the start of your last turn emits an aura of poison, causing the other enemies on their rank to take Poison damage equal to your Reflex Modifier.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 85`**

**`Haste`**`. (Passive) When you earn momentum with a weapon attack, you earn one additional momentum.`  
**`Killshot`**`. (Ability) If you critically strike before the end of your next turn, the hit deals additional damage equal to half your Reflex skill.`  
**`Assassin`**`. (Passive) Your poison damage is doubled against enemies that are below half health.`

# **MIND**

#### **`Origin Perks:`**

**`One's own Understanding`**`. (Passive) Your abilities deal extra damage equal to your Sin, up to a maximum of your Mind Modifier.`  
**`Divination`**`. (Ritual) At the start of a session, roll 2 d10's, and keep track of them. You may replace any two d10 rolls with the result of a Divination roll. This includes the tens or ones place in a d100 roll.`  
**`Summoner`**`. (Passive) Your rolls made to maintain concentration are lowered by 10.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 35`**

**`Summon Merihem`**`. (Ability, Summon, Sin +1) You summon a small demon to act as a nuisance to your enemies. The demon can use the Distraction ability, which causes attack rolls against one target to have advantage. In addition, any time that target would gain health, the amount it would gain is reduced by your Mind Modifier.`  
**`Expert`**`. (Passive) Choose one of your backgrounds. Whenever you make a roll associated with that background, your Hard Success and Critical Success ranges are increased by an amount equal to your Level + your Mind Modifier.`  
**`Black Cloud`**`. (Ability, Sin +1) You cast a shadowy cloud over an enemy and obscure their vision. Make a Mind roll, and on a success, you deal 1d10 + your Mind Modifier Dark damage. On a Hard Success, the enemy is blinded until the end of your next turn. If you fail this roll, you cannot cast this spell for the remainder of the combat.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 45`**

**`Familiar Spirits`**`. (Passive) When you have a demon summoned, your defensive rolls are lowered by an amount equal to your Mind Modifier + your Sin.`  
**`Far-sight`**`. (Passive) You can see the properties of your surrounding hexes one hex further in every direction.`  
**`Ball Lightning`**`. (Ability, Sin +1) You cast a mote of lightning toward a single target. Make a Mind roll, and on a success, you deal 2d10 + your Mind Modifier Lightning damage to one target. On a Hard Success, any enemies on that same rank take half the damage dealt to the original target. If you fail this roll, you cannot cast this spell for the remainder of the combat.`  
**`Medical Apothecary`**`. (Passive) Your knowledge of medicine helps you heal wounds. When you or your allies Patch Up or Rest, they gain a bonus to Each Recovery Die roll equal to your Mind Modifier.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 55`**

**`Summon Furies`**`. (Ability, Summon, Sin +1) You summon a swarm of demons. This swarm can use the Bottomless Pit ability. Roll Mind, and on a success, an enemy is Banished until the end of your next turn.`  
**`Packrat`**`. (Passive) You can pack more into your bags than most. You can now fit two day's worth of Food or Fuel into one inventory slot, rather than one. Food and Fuel do not stack with each other.`  
**`Druid`**`. (Ritual, Sin +1) You invoke a magical rite that forces the world to bend to your will. You give the hex you are currently in the Bountiful property.`  
**`Deal with Devils`**`. (Passive) Whenever a demon you have summoned uses their ability, you gain 1 momentum.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 65`**

**`Potestas et Principatus`**`. (Ritual, Sin +3) Once per day, you can invoke a ritual to gain more favor with the demonic hosts of Hell. In one combat of your choice before the end of the day, you can summon two demons instead of one when you use a summoning ability. They can both use their abilities on your turn.`  
**`Sacrifice Demon`**`. (Ability) You sacrifice a demon that you have summoned. When you do, that demon disappears, and you regain 2d10 + your Mind modifier health. That demon cannot be summoned for the remainder of the combat.`  
**`Frigia`**`. (Ability, Sin +1) You cast a spell that freezes the blood of an enemy. Make a Mind roll, and on a success, you deal 2d10 + your Mind Modifier Cold damage. On a Hard Success, the next weapon attack that hits them is automatically a critical hit. If you fail this roll, you cannot cast this spell for the remainder of the combat.`  
**`Field Physician`**`. (Ritual) You and all of your allies regain 3 expended Recovery Die. Once you use this Ritual, you cannot do so again until you rest in a village, town, city, or other sort of safe place.`  
**`Well Rounded`**`. (Special) Pick any two Perks with a Prerequisite of 35. These can be from any attribute.`  
 

#### **`Prerequisite: 75`**

**`Summon Furcas`**`. (Ability, Summon, Sin +1) You summon a demonic knight of Hell, which wields a flaming longsword and tower shield. Furcas can use the Hell's Phalanx ability. Roll Mind against one enemy, and on a success, the target takes 2d10 + your mind modifier Flame Damage, and allies on the near rank have advantage on their defensive rolls against weapon attacks.`  
**`Philosopher of Hell`**`. (Passive) You know much about the denizens of Hell, and they are beneath you. Any Normal Success made against a demon counts as a Hard Success.`  
**`Combat Medic`**`. (Ability) You or one ally is able to Patch Up mid-fight when you use this ability.` 

#### **`Prerequisite: 85`**

**`Summon Nephelim`**`. (Ability, Summon, Sin +2) You summon a giant Demon that was once the spawn of Hell's princes, and unbind it from the chains that held it jailed. The Nephelim can use the Wrathful Crush ability, which deals Blunt damage equal to your Mind modifier to the near rank of enemies. When this happens, roll Mind for each enemy, and on a Hard success, the enemy is bound, stunned and silenced.`  
**`Sin Shield`**`. (Ability) You turn your burden of Sin into a ward of protection. Roll Mind, and on a success, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Sin + your Mind Modifier.`  
**`Fira Infernox`**`. (Ability, Sin +2) You hurl a devastating ball of hellfire toward your enemies. Roll Mind, and on a success, you deal 3d10 + your Mind modifier Flame damage to a rank of enemies. On a hard success, roll a number of d10s equal to your mind modifier. You deal additional flame damage equal to the result. If you fail this roll, you cannot cast this spell for the remainder of the combat.`

# **VOICE**

#### **`Origin Perks`**

**`Firebrand.`** `(Passive) When you deal damage and roll a 9 or 10 on a damage die, you deal 1d10 additional Flame damage.`  
**`On a Roll.`** `(Passive) If an ally on your rank rolls a critical success, you gain 1d10 momentum immediately.`   
**`Captivating`**`. (Passive) When you enter battle, choose an ally. That ally gains 1 momentum each time you gain momentum.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 35`**

**`Persuasive`**`. (Passive) Any Voice roll made to persuade a character to see your point of view is easier. Normal Successes in this context are treated as Hard Successes.`  
**`Goad`**`. (Ability) Roll Voice, and on a success, you force an enemy that is on the far rank to move to the near rank.`  
**`Taunt`**`. (Ability) You taunt an enemy by insulting them or getting their attention. Roll Voice, and on a success, the enemy is Taunted until the end of your next turn.`  
**`Tongues of Fire`**`. (Ability) You spark an ember from your lips that burns an enemy. Roll Voice, and on a success, they take Flame damage equal to your Voice Modifier instantly, and once again at the start of your next turn.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 45`**

**`Mockery`**`. (Ability) When you use this ability, you distract one enemy and begin to mock them. If you or an ally makes a successful defensive roll against that enemy's weapon attack before the end of your next turn, the enemy takes damage equal to your Voice Modifier. The damage type is the same as the weapon attack that triggered this ability.`  
**`Merchant`**`. (Ritual) Once per day, you can either decrease the cost of a transaction or increase the gold gained from a transaction. Roll a number of d10's equal to your Voice modifier, and the price is affected by an amount equal to the result.`  
**`Good Company`**`. (Passive) When you or your allies Patch Up, two Recovery Dice can be rolled rather than only one.`  
**`Burn`**`. (Passive) When you deal Flame damage to an enemy, they take Flame damage equal to half your voice modifier at the start of your next turn. An enemy can only be affected by this once per round.` 

#### **`Prerequisite: 55`**

**`Caravaneer`**`. (Passive). You and all of your allies gain one additional inventory slot.`  
**`Appall`**`. (Ability) You say something so unhinged that it dumbfounds an enemy. Roll Voice against one enemy, and on a success, they are Dazed, and the do not contribute to the GM’s momentum pool until the end of your next turn.`  
**`Fast-talk`**`. (Ritual, +2 Sin) Once per day, you can speak with such speed and confidence that you swindle someone. While bartering, buying, or selling, you can either halve the price of an item you are trying to barter for or double the price of an item you are selling.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 65`**

**`Forked Tongue`**`. (Ritual, +1 Sin) Once per day, you can guarantee a success on a roll to deceive someone or tell a lie. You gain 1 sin when you use this, but you may gain more sin depending on how big of a lie you are telling, as the GM decides.`  
**`Rally`**`. (Maneuver) You call to your allies and grant them a second wind. When you use this maneuver, track the amount of successes you and your allies make before the end of your next turn. For each success, roll a d10. The result rolled can be distributed as regained health as you see fit between you and your allies.`  
**`Halt`**`! (Ability) You speak with authority and break the momentum of your enemies. Roll Voice, and on a success, you prevent one enemy from using maneuvers until the end of your next turn.`  
**`Warm Heart`**`. (Ritual) You can provide warmth to your allies for a short while by expending your own energy. You can expend a Recovery Die to substitute fuel for warmth. You do not gain healing from this recovery die, but provide warmth for a number of characters equal to your Voice Modifier.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 75`**

**`Pyre`**`. (Ability) You set alight the battlefield and create a hazard for your enemies. Roll Voice, and on a success, you deal 1d10 + your Voice Modifier flame damage to either the near or far rank. Any enemies that have not switched ranks to leave the burning flames by the end of your next turn take 2d10 + your Voice Modifier Flame damage.`  
**`Twice as hot, Twice as Fast`**`. (Passive) When you are below half of your maximum health, you deal 2 additional Flame damage any time you damage an enemy, and if you are in the near rank, enemies in the near rank take flame damage equal to your Voice Modifier when they target you with a weapon attack.`  
**`Heavenly Tongue`**`. (Ritual) Once per day, you can take on a supernatural knowledge of a language. Choose a language, and until your next rest, you know it if you didn't already. Any rolls made that factor in the knowledge of this language have advantage.`

#### **`Prerequisite: 85`**

**`Wealth Magnet`**`. (Passive) Any time you gain gold as a result of exploration or victory in battle, you gain 1d10 + your Voice modifier more.`  
**`Pillar of Salt`**`. (Maneuver) You utter words that ignite into a bright flash that has the power to petrify an enemy. Roll Voice against one enemy. On a success, they are both Stunned and Silenced. On a hard success, if their remaining health is below your Voice Attribute, they are turned into a pillar of salt instantly. This does, in fact, kill them.`  
**`Immolation`**`. (Ability, Concentration) You call down fire from heaven to immolate a single enemy. Roll Voice, and on a success, they take 1d10 Flame damage. At the end of your next turn, they take 2d10 Flame damage. At the end of each subsequent turn, the number of d10s increases by 1, up to a maximum of your Voice Modifier. When any of the die show a 9-10, one other enemy takes flame damage equal to that die's result. You must concentrate on this ability, and if you lose concentration, the amount of d10s you roll resets to 1 for your next cast.`

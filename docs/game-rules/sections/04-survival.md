# **HUNGER**

`Your character is a mortal, and therefore, must eat. There are three states of Hunger:`

**`Well Fed.`** `You have eaten enough to sustain you. Your Health increases by 10. You must eat a meal at least two days in a row in order to gain this state. Certain foods and perks can also provide other ways to gain this state.`  
**`Sated.`** `You are not hungry. You have eaten at least one meal in the past two days.`  
**`Hungry.`** `You have not eaten enough. If you go two days without eating, you become Hungry, and your total Health decreases by 10. It continues to decrease by 10 for every day that you do not eat a meal. If your total Health is reduced to 0 in this way, you die of starvation. Becoming Sated removes this penalty.`

## **FINDING FOOD**

`Characters can find food in a number of ways. Of course, they can purchase food in civilized areas before they set out on a journey, but if food is depleted while traveling, the Party may need to make a Find Food Roll. This is a group check in which there must be more successes than failures in order to succeed. A Find Food Roll can be made with Mind, Heart, or Reflex to see if you are able to forage for mushrooms, hunt a creature, or find a cache of edible supplies through sheer luck. On a success, you find an amount of food that is able to feed a number of characters equal to the number of successes in the Find Food Roll.` 

`Of course, there is another way: eating the corpses of those you’ve slain in battle. If you slay a common beast or animal, they can simply be harvested for food with a roll the GM deems appropriate, and on a success, enough food is harvested to feed a number of characters equal to double the creature’s size rank. However, eating a sentient creature like a humanoid or a demon is going to cause any who partake in the act to gain 5 Sin.` 

# **SURVIVING THE COLD**

`The darkened world of Fivefold is a frozen wasteland, only relieved by the small bastions of kindled shrines, volcanic vents, and occasional hot springs that are sparsely spread over the surface. When you take a rest in the wilderness, you will make a Survival Roll. This is a roll made by the whole group, in which there must be more successes than failures in order to succeed. A survival roll can be Mind, Heart, or Reflex roll to see if you can find fuel, shelter, or something else suitable to fend off the cold. If you fail this check, you may spend fuel that you already have with you to mitigate the failure and resist the cold. If you have no fuel and fail the check, you all lose at least one of your Recovery Dice. The number lost and the difficulty of the roll are determined by factors of the hex you are attempting to rest in.` 

# **INVENTORY MANAGEMENT**

`In Five Fold, managing your inventory is quite important. You can't take everything with you, and must pick and choose what to take into the wasteland. Besides the armor, clothing, and weapons that the character currently has equipped, a character can hold a number of items in their inventory equal to their Heart Modifier + their Mind Modifier. No items, except scrolls, stack. This means that a day's worth of food for one character takes up one inventory slot, and two days worth of food takes up two, etc. All Scrolls that are not slotted into gear can be stacked into one inventory slot.`

`A character's inventory size can be increased with certain perks and by having a mount. Mounts can hold a number of items equal to their size rank.`

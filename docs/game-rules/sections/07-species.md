# SPECIES

`This section lists all the playable species in Fivefold. Each grants unique benefits and features.`

# **HUMANS**

`Humans have fended for themselves for centuries, with their kingdoms being isolated across what was once a great sea to the north. They were once a seafaring people, with raiding fleets so powerful that the north was bled dry by their excursions. Now, in the sunless freeze, their mighty vessels have been captured in ship graveyards, frozen into the ice fields that bridge the gap between the mainland and their home. It was only recently that humans began to migrate south toward the rest of the world, as their homeland has turned violent and feudal, with warlords competing for limited resources in their ancestral homeland.`

`Humans gain the following features:`  
**`Versatile.`** `Start with +5 to any two stats.`  
**`Resilient.`** `A Human’s maximum health increases by an amount equal to half their heart modifier * their level. This change is retroactive, meaning if their Heart modifier increases, they gain the additional maximum health.`

#  **SINISTRE ELVES**

`At the end of the last era, the Sinistre Elves ruled most of the known world. Their empire stretched to every corner of the realm, and they were responsible for many of the cultural and technological advances that the world saw. However, a series of terrible emperors, constant warring at their borders and internal revolts plagued the Empire. When the sun set for the last time, the Empire fell shortly after.`  
`Sinistre Elves are pale-skinned with white or silvery hair and red eyes. They are descendents of cave dwelling elves that lived underground for so long that they became albino, though that was so far in the past that only the oldest orders of scholars and historians care to document and research their underground origins. Some orders wish to search for their ancestral homes to return there in the wake of this sunless future, while other factions believe that the time to unite and reconquer the world is now, and the sun can no longer reign as a tyrant and sting their albino skin.`  
`Culturally, Sinistre Elves value arts and philosophy. Sculpture, painting, architecture and writing are all thought to be divinely appointed arts that are greatly respected among the elves, and many of the wealthy houses will happily dish out their riches to purchase the finest works or commission masterpieces that honor them. Many Sinistre elves found outside of Sinestra are on quests to procure special stone for monuments or highly pigmented materials for a new painting.` 

`Sinistre Elves gain the following features:`  
**`Naturally Brilliant:`** `Start with +10 to Mind.`  
**`Renaissance-born:`** `Sinistre Elves start with a second Origin perk of their choice.` 

# **HARES**

`(Flavor Text)`  
`Hares gain the following features:`  
**`Faithful:`** `Start with +10 to Soul.`  
**`Lucky Rabbit:`** `A Hare starts with a pool of points called “Luck”. Luck begins at 50, and can be spent to lower any roll. When you do this, you take a number of points from your luck pool equal to the amount you want to lower the roll by. Luck is restored by an amount equal to your level whenever you naturally roll 10 or below. Luck’s maximum is 100.`

# **SPHINXITES**

`(Flavor Text)`  
`Sphinxites gain the following features:`  
**`Cat-like Reflexes.`** `Start with +10 Reflex.`  
**`Night-vision.`** `Sphinxites can see in the dark as if it were normally lit, but can only see in shades of grey in those conditions.`  
**`Nine-Lives.`** `Once per day, if a Sphinxite is reduced to 0 health, they are instead reduced to 1.` 

# **CORVIDS**

`(Flavor Text)`  
`Corvids gain the following features:`  
**`Charming.`** `Start with +10 Voice.`  
**`Mimicry.`** `Corvids can perfectly mimic any voice they have heard before. They cannot utter things they have not heard said in that voice, though, and must use repeat words.`  
**`Affinity for Shiny things.`** `When a Corvid finds gold, they find additional gold equal to their level.`

# **SABRE-TOOTH ELVES**

`(Flavor Text)`  
`Sabre-Tooth Elves gain the following features:`  
**`Sturdy.`** `Start with +10 Heart.`  
**`Feral Bite.`** `When you reach your Momentum threshold, you can attempt to bite an enemy. Your bite counts as a Heart Weapon with the Near range that deals Sharp damage equal to half your Heart Modifier + your level on a hit. This is not a maneuver, but is used alongside any maneuver you would like to use.` 

# **CAPRICA**

`Caprica are a species of humanoids that possess goat, bovine or sheep-like features. Hooved feet, velvety fur over much of their body, and horns make this species particularly hard to miss. Most range in size from 6’0” to 8’0”. They are regarded by most to be cursed or in association with demons due to their appearance, though there is little to suggest truth to this. Even so, some Caprica form covens and cohort with demons for protection against persecution, which only encourages the false perceptions.`  
`Male Caprica are goat-faced and much larger than their female counterparts. They typically take on more animalistic features and resemble large, bipedal rams. Female Caprica resemble human women with smaller, curled horns than the males, rounded lamb-ears, and cloven hooves for feet. Caprica believe that the males are responsible for shouldering the burden of sin for their tribe, with the first born males of each clan often ritualistically exiled, to carry the sins of their family into the wilderness. Many of these exiles die, but those that survive often take up the mantle as adventurer or cutthroat.` 

`Capricas gain the following features:`  
**`False Perception.`** `Start with a -5 to Voice, and a +15 to one other stat.`   
**`Slow Metabolism.`** `When you would drop from one hunger stage to the next, Roll Heart. On a Hard Success, you remain at your current stage.`   
**`Wool Coat.`** `Your Survival Rolls are lowered by an amount equal to your Heart Modifier + your Level.`

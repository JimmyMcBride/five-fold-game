# **CONDITIONS**

`This is a list of conditions that characters can be affected by.`

* **`Banished`**`. A Banished creature cannot be targeted by anything, is immune to all damage, and is Silenced and Stunned.`  
* **`Blinded.`** `A Blinded character makes all sight-based Rolls (weapon attacks, some defensive rolls, ability rolls, etc) with disadvantage.`   
* **`Bound`**`. A Bound creature cannot switch ranks or flee. Weapon attacks against bound creatures have advantage.`  
* **`Dazed`**`. A Dazed creature can use either an action or ability on their turn, but not both. Weapon attacks against dazed creatures have advantage.`  
* **`Hidden`**`. A hidden creature cannot be targeted by weapon attacks or abilities that only affect one target. Also, they have advantage on weapon attacks.`  
* **`Paralyzed.`** `A paralyzed creature is Silenced, Stunned, and Bound, and fails all rolls that rely on movement (defensive rolls against weapon attacks, dodging certain abilities, etc).`  
* **`Silenced`**`. A Silenced creature cannot use abilities.`  
* **`Stunned`**`. A stunned creature cannot make weapon attacks.`

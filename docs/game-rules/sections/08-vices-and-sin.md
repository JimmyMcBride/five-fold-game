# VICES & SIN

`When you create a character, choose their Vice from one of the seven deadly sins: Envy, Gluttony, Greed, Lust, Pride, Sloth, Wrath. These grant your character both a positive and a negative effect, and generate Sin in different ways.` 

## **SIN**

`Sin is something each player character accrues over time while adventuring. It can be utilized by enemies, grant penalties to rolls when the GM indicates it, and can even be used by Occultists to cast spells. Sin is accrued in the ways indicated by your character's vice, and by performing actions that the Celestial Council has deemed as unholy. Usually, this includes the Holy Tenets:`

*`Thou shall not steal.`*  
*`Thou shall not murder.`*  
*`Thou shall not bear false witness.`*  
*`Thou shall not cohort with the demonic.`*  
*`Thou shall not practice dark magick.`*

`Of course, the GM is allowed to decide that any action a player character takes is morally dubious, and thus, would accrue Sin. If there is a disagreement or if the action is in a grey area, the players may vote on whether or not the action was sinful.` 

## **OVERCOME BY SIN**

`If a character's Sin is ever higher than their total Recovery Dice, they are at risk of being overcome by their sin and being demon possessed. If they begin Dying under these conditions, they must roll Soul each time they lose a recovery die. On a failure, the character loses a second Recovery Die. If a character dies under these conditions, they are immediately overcome by their Sin, and are transformed into a demon that is of the type of their vice. The demonized character becomes controlled by the GM, and the player should say their goodbyes and roll up a new character.`

## **FORGIVENESS OF SIN**

`Sin can be cleansed from characters by performing certain actions. These include, but are not limited to:`

* **`Pilgrimage`**`. In the world of Five Fold, there are various shrines and holy places that can be visited and prayed at in order to remove sin.`  
* **`Abilities and Perks`**`. Certain abilities can remove sin.`  
* **`Slaying Demons`**`. Slaying any demon removes a number of Sin, as listed in their stat block as Sin Absolved. All characters present in the combat encounter have their sin absolved by the amount. A character can refuse the benefit, if they wish.`

# THE 7 VICES

# **LUST**

`Lust in Fivefold is about using intimacy, appearance, and affection manipulatively or compulsively. Lust is the hollow performance of affection for power, safety, or validation.` 

**`You gain Sin from Lust when you:`**

* `Use your charm, beauty, or sexuality to gain power, advantage, or influence over another person (this includes seduction, manipulation, or trading intimacy for protection/favor).`  
* `Pursue or indulge in pleasure while others suffer. (e.g., feasting, resting, or flirting in the aftermath of tragedy or battle).`  
* `Create or deepen romantic or sexual bonds with someone you don’t respect, care about, or intend to honor.`

*`Examples:`*   
*`You seduce a noble to gain access to their manor → Sin +1`*  
*`You try to distract a guard with flirtation to let your allies sneak past → Sin +1`*

*`You spend a night with a traveler just to steal their gear the next morning → Sin +1`*  
*`You flirt with another PC’s companion just to provoke them → Sin +1`*  
*`You take a bath and sip wine while your wounded party members patch each other up → Sin +1`*  
   
**`Honeyed Words`**`. When you make a roll that relies on charm or attraction, the roll is lowered by 10. (E.G., bribing a noble with physical favors, distracting a guard using your body, flirting with someone to bargain.)`

**`Restless Nights`**`. You cannot roll Recovery Dice during a rest unless a character who could be a romantic or sexual partner is resting nearby. If they are a PC or retainer, you must roll Soul, Heart, or Mind, and on a failure, you gain 1 Sin. You do not necessarily act upon your urges, but it is indicative of your proclivity to promiscuity.`

**`Touch of Pleasure`**`. While you have 5 Sin or more, you restore additional hit points equal to your Sin when you heal someone or are healed. However, you become far easier to tempt, and must roll Mind or Soul to resist any invitation of pleasure, no matter how inconvenient or dangerous.`

# **WRATH**

`Wrath in Fivefold is not righteous anger, but rage unbound by wisdom. It is the refusal to forgive, the need to punish, or the thirst for violence even when peace is possible. Wrath tempts the strong with false justice and confused mercy for weakness.` 

**`You gain Sin from Wrath when you:`**

* `Inflict violence upon a helpless, surrendered, or incapacitated creature. (Mercy is a choice—and you refused.)`  
* `Continue fighting when surrender, retreat, or mercy is clearly an option. (Even if others beg for peace.)`  
* `Harm or endanger an ally due to your rage, impatience, or unwillingness to listen.`

*`Examples:`*  
*`You execute a foe that has been bound and silenced → Sin +1`*  
*`You keep attacking an enemy who that cannot fight back → Sin +1`*  
*`You attack someone simply because they insulted you, or attack an enemy that Taunted you→ Sin +1`*  
*`You provoke an unnecessary duel in a public place → Sin +1`*  
*`You ignore a call to retreat because “they started it” → Sin +1`*  
   
**`Furious Precision.`** `Your weapon attack rolls are lowered by 5.`

**`Wreckless`**`. Your Defensive Rolls are increased by 5.`

**`Collateral Damage.`** `While you have 5 Sin or more, your critical hits deal extra damage equal to your Sin. However, landing a critical hit causes one ally in the near rank to take damage equal to your Sin, as well.` 

# **GREED**

`Greed. The insatiable desire to claim, hoard, and dominate. It is an entitlement to possess what others lack. Those with the vice of Greed believe that they deserve more, even at the expense of their companions. It twists charity into foolishness, and sacrifice into folly. Greed weighs every soul in its potential for material gain.`

**`You gain Sin from Greed when you:`**

* `Claim a reward, item, or resource that could have gone to an ally, despite knowing it would help them more.`  
* `Hoard or hide resources from your allies, including food, gear, healing items, or currency.`  
* `Exploit others for material gain, even if they are allies, innocents, or dependents.`

*`Examples:`*  
*`You keep a powerful weapon for yourself, despite another character needing it more → Sin +1`*  
*`You loot a corpse while your companions are still fighting nearby → Sin +1`*  
*`You sell off shared treasure without consulting the group → Sin +1`*  
*`You refuse to share rations with a starving NPC or PC unless compensated → Sin +1`*  
*`You steal from a traveling merchant who offered you shelter → Sin +1`*  
   
**`Avarice Inventory`**`. You have one additional inventory slot.` 

**`Miser's Grip`**`. When you want to give away or discard any wealth or item, roll Mind or Soul. If you fail this roll, you gain 1 Sin.` 

**`The Weight of Wealth`**`. While you have 5 Sin or more, you gain 2 additional Inventory slots. However, if your inventory has less than half its slots filled, you suffer a penalty to all rolls equal to your sin.` 

# **SLOTH**

`Sloth is spiritual decay. It is the quiet erosion of one's own will, the surrender of duty, and the refusal to rise when called. Sloth tempts inaction with the lie that your effort is wasted, that action is too dangerous, and that someone else will bear the burden. Sloth drapes itself in weariness, masking cowardice as caution and indifference as wisdom. Neutrality is a dangerous path when evil rules the dark of night.`

**`You gain Sin from Sloth when you:`**

* `Avoid a responsibility, danger, or duty that is clearly yours, especially when others must act in your place.`  
* `Fail to act in a moment of need, including refusing to aid an ally or intervene in suffering.`  
* `Abstain from progress—intentionally delaying decisions, resting excessively, or allowing evil to flourish through inaction.`

*`Examples:`*  
*`You let a character suffer (either by taking damage, being punished, or losing something important to them) when you could have prevented it → Sin +1`*  
*`You exhibit apathy regarding a crucial decision, leaving it entirely to other characters to resolve when your input would be valuable → Sin +1`*  
*`You pass on investigating a potential danger to rest instead → Sin +1`*  
*`You take no action or ability for two turns in a row. → Sin +1`*  
*`You rest while Well Fed → Sin +1`*  
   
**`Immovable Inertia`**`. If you take no action or ability on your turn, you gain +5 Momentum and +1 Sin.`

**`Slow to Stir`**`. Except for Immovable Inertia, your Actions, Passives, and Abilities do not give you any momentum on your first turn in combat.`

**`Watch the World Burn`**`. While you have 5 Sin or more, you have advantage on Defensive rolls if you did not take an Action or Ability on your previous turn. However, each time you use a Maneuver, your enemies gain 1 momentum die for Every 5 sin you have.` 

# **GLUTTONY**

`Gluttony is an unquenchable hunger for every pleasure the world can offer. Over-eating, over-indulging, ever seeking to sate the void of hunger for all that can be and will be consumed. It represents a lack of restraint that fuels temporary strength and indulgence, but ultimately at the expense of spiritual fortitude and self control. When you give in to Gluttony, you blind yourself to the need for balance. In this sin, the body may swell with excess, but the spirit grows sluggish and prone to decay.`

**`You gain Sin from Gluttony when you:`**

* `Consume resources (food, healing potions, or restorative items) in excess of what is necessary for survival or immediate need.`  
* `Prioritize lavish feasts or excessive banqueting over conserving sustenance for the party’s long-term welfare.`  
* `Use food or consumable resources as a crutch in lieu of strategic action, even when your condition is stable.`  
* `Refuse to share or ration edible resources when allies are in need or facing scarcity.`  
* `Engage in extravagant dining or indulgent behavior, turning necessity into an opulent display that detracts from duty or focus.`

*`Examples`*  
*`You attend a lavish event and take part in a feast → Sin +1`*  
*`You use a consumable resource to restore your hit points and over-heal → Sin +1`*  
*`You consume food on a day where at least one of your companions does not → Sin +1`*  
*`You become Well Fed when others in your party are only Sated → Sin +1`*  
*`You hoard food or consumable items, having more on your person than anyone else in your party → Sin +1`*  
   
**`Gorged Vitality`**`. While you are Well Fed, you regain 2 more health from all healing.` 

**`Insatiable Hunger`**`. When you are Well Fed, if you do not eat a meal for a day, you immediately become Hungry, rather than Sated.` 

**`Endless Feast`**`. While you have 5 Sin or more, your weapon attacks deal additional damage equal to your sin as long as you are Well Fed. While you are hungry, your rolls suffer disadvantage, as you enter into withdrawals from your typical indulgence.` 

# **ENVY**

`Envy is the corrosive habit of measuring your worth solely against the achievements and possessions of others. The constant comparison of yourself to others erodes your judgment and often forces you to mimic or overcompensate for what you believe you lack. When you give in to Envy, you begin to lose sight of your own path, letting the success of others dictate your choices, sometimes at the cost of your own identity. Envy sees miracles as unfair, allies as rivals, and turns shared joy into hatred.`

**`You gain Sin from Envy when you:`**

* `Deliberately choose an ability, perk, or tactic solely because an ally has taken a similar action.`  
* `Make decisions based on comparison rather than merit.`  
* `Allow your judgment to be clouded by the prospect of appearing inferior.`  
* `Sabotage a character after they experienced a victory.`  
* `Routinely imitate another character’s actions or mannerisms to duplicate success.`

*`Examples:`*  
*`You pick a perk that another ally has → Sin +1`*  
*`You use the same kind of weapon in a combat encounter that another ally is wielding → Sin +1`*  
*`You spread gossip about a character that is successful or beloved → Sin +1`*  
*`You do anything with the intention of being seen as the best at something → Sin +1`*  
   
**`Mirror the Best.`** `Once per day, you can use an action or ability that another player character has.` 

**`Hesitant Aid`**`. Before you use an ability, action, or maneuver that would buff an ally, you must roll Mind or Soul. If you fail, you gain +1 Sin.`

**`Codependent Rivalry`**`. While you have 5 Sin or more, you form a dependence on your comparisons to others. Choose one ally. Whenever they receive a buff, you also receive the same buff. However, for any stat in which your value is lower than your chosen ally’s, rolls using that stat suffer disadvantage.` 

# **PRIDE**

`Pride is the exaltation of the self at the cost of wisdom, humility, and connection. It manifests as arrogance, as isolation, as the refusal to admit weakness. The prideful do not like to ask for help, or share glory. Pride tells you that you are enough for any job, that you are chosen, that you are the one who must stand above all obstacles and conquer them. It turns every victory into entitlement and every failure into shame.` 

**`You gain Sin from Pride when you:`**

* `Refuse material assistance, healing, or buffs from an ally, even when it would help you significantly.`  
* `Insist on taking the lead or credit for actions that could have been shared with others.`  
* `Ignore advice, warnings, or signs out of self confidence.`  
* `Act alone in dangerous or strategic moments.`  
* `Use your own abilities or rituals in place of more effective options offered by others.`

*`Examples:`*  
*`You refuse healing from an ally, saying "I can handle it." → Sin +1`*  
*`You attack an enemy that inflicted you with the Taunted debuff → Sin +1`*  
*`You dismiss the warnings or knowledge of an ally regarding an enemy, seeking instead to find your own strategy → Sin +1`*  
*`You reframe a group success as your own personal triumph during dialogue with an NPC → Sin +1`*  
*`You decline to participate in a group ritual or collaborative effort because “you don’t need them” → Sin +1`*  
   
**`Solo Glory`**`. If you target an enemy that wasn't targeted by any other character since the end of the enemy's last turn, you gain 2 momentum.`

**`No One's Help`**`. If an ally attempts to grant you a buff or heal you, roll Mind or Soul. On a failure, you gain 1 Sin.` 

**`Self-Exalting`**`. While you have 5 Sin or more, buffs and heals that you apply to yourself are doubled in strength. However, if any enemy taunts you, you take damage equal to your Heart Modifier.`

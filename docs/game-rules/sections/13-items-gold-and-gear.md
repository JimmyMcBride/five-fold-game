# ITEMS, GOLD, & GEAR

`What you carry on you will often make the difference between life or death in the endless night. This section covers the rules and costs for weapons, armor, and various other kinds of equipment that a traveling character might want on their person.` 

# **GOLD**

`The most common form of currency is the gold coin, minted by the Sinistre Elf Empire several centuries ago. While they are not produced any longer, there were enough in circulation at the time of the empire’s fall that they still remain the primary coin for commerce, and are accepted almost everywhere. A character can hold up to 500 gold coins in one inventory slot.` 

## **Bank Notes**

`Thankfully, banks do exist in the world of Fivefold, though they are often only seen in larger cities. A bank can exchange any amount of gold for a banknote of equal value, and all banknotes are consolidated into one item that does not take up an inventory slot. When you want to barter using your bank note, a merchant will simply mark the gold they accepted or gave to you in the transaction, and you will do the same on their bank note, while exchanging some important information between each other. However, the further away from a city with a bank you are, the less likely anyone is willing to accept a bank note as currency. If you are no more than 8 hours of travel away from a major city, notes are generally accepted. Between 9 and 24 hours away, you will typically need to make a Voice roll to barter with a bank note. Further than that, and the Voice roll will need to be a hard success.` 

`While a one-to-one conversion to modern day currency is not exact, a good rule of thumb is to think of one gold coin as being worth roughly five US dollars. This rule of thumb will help come up with a price for something that is not listed in this book, should that come up in play.`

# **ARMOR**

`Armor comes in three different categories: Cloth, Light, or Heavy. Cloth armors are things like Magi robes, and travel clothing, and offer little protection, but have more slots for Scrolls than other types. Light armors are things like leather, padded gambisons, or half-plate, and each piece of Light armor worn decreases Defensive Reflex rolls by 1, making you more likely to avoid damage. Heavy armors are things like full plate or refined chitin, and each piece of Heavy Armor worn decreases your Defensive Heart rolls by 1. Level 1 characters start with an armor piece for each body part.`

# **UNIQUE ARMOR & WEAPONS**

`Sometimes, adventurers will be able to find or even craft special armor and weapons that may enhance or alter their power in combat. Below is a list of this gear, including the typical methods on how to obtain them.` 

#### **`ERUDITE`**

`Erudite is an ore that can be found in cavernous burrows deep in Volcanic wastelands. It was once hardened magma that was shed from the Relgath the Igneous Scorpion. Erudite can be forged into heavy armor, certain light armors, and weapons, as long as they are mostly made of metal.`   
**`Erudite Armor.`** `Each piece of Erudite armor worn has one less slot, but reduces your damage taken from Sharp and Blunt by 1. When you are wearing a full set of Erudite armor, you gain a new maneuver:`   
***`Igneous Obsidian. (Maneuver)`** `Upon using this maneuver, you gain temporary health equal to your level + your Heart Modifier. When a creature reduces the temporary health to 0, you deal flame damage equal to your level + your Heart modifier to them.`*  
**`Erudite Weapons.`** `Erudite weapons have one less slot, deal flame damage, and ignore fire resistance.`

#### **`REQUIEM STORM`**

`There is a monastery, somewhere out in the world, called the Abbey of the Storm’s Eye. The monastic order that resides here is very small, but they craft gear that is infused with the power of the blizzards that so often buffet their home. Crafting gear using the tools at this abbey will produce Requiem Storm armor and weapons.`  
**`Requiem Storm Armor.`** `Each piece of Requiem Storm armor has one less slot, but increases your critical range for weapons and abilities by 2. When you are wearing a full set of Requiem Storm armor, you gain the following passive:`  
	`Whirlwind. (Passive) Your critical weapon attacks cause a whirlwind to surround the target. The whirlwind deals 2 Lightning damage at the start of the target's turn, and lasts for the remainder of the combat encounter. You can cause more whirlwinds to stack on the target with more critical hits. Hitting a target with Lightning damage from an Ability will detonate the whirlwinds violently, and cause them to deal 1d10 damage per whirlwind on the target, which removes all stacks.`

#### **`WAND OF HELLFIRE`**

`This is a wand that permanently has one Flame Scroll slotted into it, and deals entirely flame damage. When the wand is found, it has 1d10 + 1 charges. A charge can be expended as an action to turn about 5 cubic feet of stone into lava. The charges do not regenerate.`

# **SCROLLS**

When these scrolls are slotted into a piece of equipment, they grant the wearer or wielder a boost to their power, as the words of GRDN are alive. Scrolls come in different tiers, as well, representing just how well preserved the fragments of parchments are.

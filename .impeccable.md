## Design Context

### Users

Fivefold is for players in browser-based, text-first dungeon runs. They need to read unfolding fiction, move through rooms, track character and combat state, parse narration logs, collect loot, and make tactical choices without visual fatigue.

### Brand Personality

Grim, focused, oppressive. The interface should feel like a dangerous instrument for play, not a generic fantasy website.

### Aesthetic Direction

Player-facing grim dark fantasy with dread and focus. The UI should support text-heavy, turn-based dungeon play while feeling ritualistic, severe, and distinctive. Avoid parchment panels, gold trim, ornate MMO styling, and generic fantasy RPG tropes.

### Current Design-System Direction

The official direction is **Obsidian + Bone**, a tomb-ledger console: volcanic charcoal shell, graphite panels, bone text, tallow command accents, clot danger states, verdigris success states, and severe manuscript typography. Treat `/` as the bootstrap specimen for this official theme, not a marketing page.

`DESIGN.md` is the root, AI-consumable design-system source for this official theme.

### Design Principles

- Preserve readability first; atmosphere cannot interfere with long-session play.
- Let typography, spacing, and material texture carry the fantasy tone.
- Use scarce, meaningful accent color for danger, holiness, corruption, and command state.
- Prefer dense but organized tactical surfaces over decorative layouts.
- Keep `/` as the playable-shell specimen until production run flows replace it.
